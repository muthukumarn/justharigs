FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about our products.

Is there a free plan or trial?

Seatable has a permanent free tier (1 event, 5 tables, 25 guests). PureStrength Coach offers a 30-day free trial on all paid plans — no credit card required to start.

Can I sign in with Google or Yahoo?

Yes. Both apps support Google OAuth and Yahoo OAuth sign-in. You can also use email + password if you prefer.

Does PureStrength sync to my calendar?

Yes. Trainers can connect Google Calendar or Yahoo Calendar in Settings. Every session you schedule is automatically added as a calendar event. You can disconnect at any time.

What is the referral program in PureStrength?

Share your unique referral link with other trainers. When they sign up and subscribe, you both get 50% off your next paid month. No limit on referrals.

Can guests find their table without logging in?

Yes. Seatable generates a QR code when you publish your event. Guests scan it and type their name — no account needed. Works on any phone.

Can I collaborate with a co-host on a Seatable event?

Yes. Invite up to 2 co-hosts from the Co-hosts tab inside your event. Co-hosts can edit participants, manage seating, and publish the event.

How do I cancel a subscription?

Cancel any time from Account Settings → Manage Billing inside the app. You keep access until the end of your current billing period — no questions asked.

Where is my data stored?

All data is stored in AWS DynamoDB in the US-East-2 (Ohio) region with HTTPS encryption in transit. We never sell your data or use it for advertising.
