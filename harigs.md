# Harigs LLC — Registration Guide
**State:** Georgia · **County:** Forsyth · **City:** Cumming

---

## Step 1 — Choose and verify your business name

- Full legal name: **Harigs LLC**
- Georgia requires the name to include "LLC", "L.L.C.", or "Limited Liability Company"
- Check name availability at the Georgia Secretary of State eCorp portal:
  https://ecorp.sos.ga.gov/
- Search "Harigs" to confirm no existing entity conflicts

---

## Step 2 — Appoint a Registered Agent

Georgia law requires every LLC to have a registered agent with a **physical street address in Georgia** (no PO Boxes). The agent must be available during normal business hours to receive legal notices.

**Options:**
- **Yourself** (if you have a Georgia address) — free
- **A friend/family member** at a Georgia address — free
- **Commercial registered agent service** — typically $50–$150/year
  - Examples: Northwest Registered Agent, Registered Agents Inc., ZenBusiness

You will need the agent's full name and street address during filing.

---

## Step 3 — File Articles of Organization (the main filing)

**Where:** Georgia Secretary of State eCorp portal
**URL:** https://ecorp.sos.ga.gov/
**Fee:** $100 (one-time, payable by credit/debit card)

**What you'll need to enter:**
- LLC name: Harigs LLC
- Principal office address (can be your home address)
- Registered agent name and address (from Step 2)
- Organizer name and address (that's you — Muthu)
- Effective date (choose "immediately" unless you have a reason to delay)

Processing is typically **same day to 2 business days** online.

You will receive a **Certificate of Organization** via email — save this PDF, you'll need it for the bank account.

---

## Step 4 — Get an EIN (Employer Identification Number)

The EIN is your business's tax ID, like a Social Security number for the company. Required for a business bank account, taxes, and paying contractors.

**Where:** IRS website — free, takes ~5 minutes
**URL:** https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/apply-for-an-employer-identification-number-ein-online

- Select "Limited Liability Company" as entity type
- Select "1 member" (single-member LLC) unless you have a partner
- Reason for applying: "Started a new business"
- You'll get the EIN immediately on screen — write it down

---

## Step 5 — Create an Operating Agreement

Not legally required in Georgia but **strongly recommended**. It:
- Establishes you as the sole owner
- Protects your limited liability status
- Required by most banks to open a business account

For a single-member LLC, a simple 1–2 page document is fine. You can:
- Use a free template from Northwest Registered Agent or LegalZoom
- Have an attorney draft one (~$150–$300)

Key things to include: member name, ownership %, how profits are distributed, management structure, dissolution process.

---

## Step 6 — Forsyth County / City of Cumming business license

Depending on whether your address is inside Cumming city limits or unincorporated Forsyth County:

**If inside Cumming city limits:**
- Contact: City of Cumming Business License Office
- Phone: (770) 781-2010
- Address: 100 Main St, Cumming, GA 30040
- Fee: varies by business type, typically $75–$100/year

**If in unincorporated Forsyth County:**
- Contact: Forsyth County Tax Commissioner or Probate Court
- Phone: (770) 781-2101
- Some home-based businesses are exempt — confirm with the county

For a software/online business operating from home, the requirement is often minimal or waived. Worth a quick call to confirm.

---

## Step 7 — Open a business bank account

Take these documents to the bank:
1. Certificate of Organization (from Step 3)
2. EIN confirmation letter (from Step 4)
3. Operating Agreement (from Step 5)
4. Your government-issued ID

**Good options for small LLCs:**
- **Mercury** (mercury.com) — online, no fees, great for tech startups
- **Relay** (relayfi.com) — online, no fees, good for small businesses
- **Chase Business Checking** — in-person, good if you want a branch nearby
- **Wells Fargo** — has a branch in Cumming if you prefer in-person

Keep all business income and expenses through this account only — critical for liability protection.

---

## Step 8 — Annual renewal with Georgia Secretary of State

Georgia LLCs must file an **Annual Registration** each year:
- **When:** January 1 – April 1 (first renewal is the year after you form)
- **Fee:** $50/year
- **Where:** https://ecorp.sos.ga.gov/
- Missing this deadline results in administrative dissolution

Set a calendar reminder for January each year.

---

## Step 9 — Georgia state taxes

For a software/SaaS business:
- **No sales tax** on software-as-a-service in Georgia (as of 2024) — confirm with a CPA if unsure
- **Income tax:** Single-member LLC is a "disregarded entity" — profits flow to your personal tax return (Schedule C), no separate LLC tax return needed
- **If you hire employees:** Register with Georgia Department of Revenue for withholding
